Profitability and business KPI definitions

Revenue requires posted or otherwise qualified sales actuals; gross profit is revenue minus attributable cost of goods sold; operating profit also deducts qualified operating expenses. Working capital requires qualified current assets and current liabilities. Cash runway requires qualified cash and burn rate. Stock turnover, production yield, purchase variance, receivables ageing and payroll require their respective canonical transaction records. None can be calculated from mere counts of status records, employee activity or unapproved engineering BOMs. Missing and incomplete figures must remain unavailable, never zero.
