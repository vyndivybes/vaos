# Sixteen-employee prelaunch training and qualification

13 October 2026. Qualification campaign, not a launch certificate.

## What has been exercised

Run `npm run test:workforce-prelaunch` for all sixteen identities. Each exercises
its declared task admission, denial of ungranted evidence deletion, ordinary
action denial while training, and action denial while restricted. The store is
isolated in memory. No model learning, source retrieval, effect execution,
qualification-table update or production promotion occurs in these exercises.

The Knowledge graph-read drill exposed a missing action policy. The correction
admits its already declared ANALYSE capability as PREPARE_ONLY, without executing
a graph mutation or enabling a business write.

## Launch acceptance contract

Recorded Q levels and ACTIVE labels are insufficient. For each intended launch
capability, retain current-revision evidence for these proposed campaign gates:

1. Three distinct representative source-backed role exercises with independently
   checked expected results. Synthetic and live evidence must remain labelled.
2. Missing/stale/conflicting source and out-of-authority exercises fail safely.
   All applicable safety cases must pass. Unsupported tasks must remain explicit.
3. Retry, duplicate delivery, cancellation and recovery cause no duplicate effect
   or premature successful result. Verify durable readback where applicable.
4. A distinct reviewer checks source authority, output, evidence and claimed scope.
5. Signed-in normal intake, dispatch and handoff are tested with the actual deployed
   service. A database-seeded fixture does not qualify this journey.
6. Fresh runtime activity meets the role's approved SLA over consecutive scheduled
   cycles. Readiness reports show stale activity rather than refreshing timestamps.
7. A scoped assessment and its evidence references are persisted through existing
   governed qualification transitions. Human release approval follows all gates.

These thresholds are the campaign's acceptance proposal. They do not replace
existing policy or broaden permissions. Prepare-only qualification remains
prepare-only. Production effect capabilities require their separate commissioning.

the Orchestrator. Eight operational controllers have VYNDI_PREPARE_ONLY_GOVERNANCE
assessments dated 8 October. In the checked execution-evidence and handoff tables,
only Production has verified activity in the last 24 hours. Five employees own
blocked work, totalling six work packages. The approvals table has zero PENDING
records, although static runtime labels show three approval-state agents.

The deployed supervision page returned LANGGRAPH_SOURCE_OR_EXECUTION_UNAVAILABLE.
Local supervision tests cannot qualify that deployed integration.

The separate Stage 9 remote procurement sandbox report records synthetic remote
acceptance, replay and recovery. It used an isolated signing key and an acceptance
runner, not the live production VAOS sender. Do not count it as production
procurement authority or evidence for the other fifteen roles.

## Remaining operational qualification

Repair and live-verify supervision; reconcile static runtime labels with durable
state; resolve authoritative-source and mission dependencies; execute the role
exercises through normal governed intake; perform current scoped assessments and
independent review. The launch decision remains HOLD until these are evidenced.

No qualification labels, assessments or heartbeat timestamps should be stamped
directly in SQL to make this checklist pass.
