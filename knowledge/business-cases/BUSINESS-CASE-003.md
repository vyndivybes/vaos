# BUSINESS-CASE-003 — VYNDI carbon endurance/gravel bicycle market entry
**Research date:** 2026-11-06 | **Status:** DECISION SUPPORT / UNAPPROVED / READ-ONLY
**Scope:** India-led Vāyú Shastr (VYNDI) manufacturing entry, with a German gravel-market demand signal as *external context only*.

## Executive conclusion
**Conditional pilot; defer owned-factory CAPEX.** Industry cost evidence makes a purely price-led competition against China/Taiwan risky. A differentiated, independently validated endurance/gravel platform, lean supplier network, low inventory, evidence-backed pricing, and milestone-based order commitments should be tested before fixed-capital expansion. This is a management hypothesis, not a supplier-quoted cost or market forecast.

## Opportunity and competitive structure
- **Customer hypothesis A — premium endurance riders (India):** value fit, ride quality, quality assurance, after-sales and transparent engineering; needs buyer interviews and actual willingness-to-pay evidence.
- **Customer hypothesis B — endurance/gravel riders:** choose tire clearance, comfort, all-road functionality and serviceability; Germany is a demand signal, not transferable Indian TAM.
- **Customer hypothesis C — custom/OEM frameset engineering:** technical quality-control, layup engineering and white-label development; requires commercial contract and production-capability verification.
- **Competitive alternatives:** imported China/Taiwan OEM/ODM frames, established global-brand bicycles, Indian bicycle manufacturers and custom builders. **No brand-by-brand SKU pricing, import duty or delivered-cost comparison is qualified in this edition**; collect comparable current quotations before positioning.

## Illustrative unit economics — NOT a VYNDI costed BOM
Every amount below is a **hypothetical input for sensitivity analysis**, not a verified estimate, supplier quote or management budget.

| Model input | Assumption |
|---|---:|
| Frameset selling price | ₹55,000 |
| Fully variable unit cost assumption | ₹36,000 |
| Unit contribution before fixed costs | ₹19,000 |
| Fixed costs per month | ₹2,00,000 |
| Contribution margin | 34.55% |
| Operating break-even | **11 framesets/month** |

| Scenario | Units/month assumed | Revenue | Variable cost | Fixed cost | Monthly operating surplus |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Bear | 6 | ₹3,30,000 | ₹2,16,000 | ₹2,00,000 | **–₹86,000** |
| Base | 12 | ₹6,60,000 | ₹4,32,000 | ₹2,00,000 | **₹28,000** |
| Bull | 20 | ₹11,00,000 | ₹7,20,000 | ₹2,00,000 | **₹1,80,000** |

**Cost sensitivity:** If variable unit cost is ₹45,000 at the same ₹55,000 selling price, unit contribution falls to ₹10,000 and break-even increases to **20 framesets per month**. The hypothetical base case at 12 units then loses ₹80,000/month. This demonstrates why landed BOM and warranty reserve verification precede launch commitments.

**Exclusions:** GST/tax treatment, working-capital timing, factory CAPEX, mold/tool amortization, financing, returns, warranty, test failures, customs, sales-channel commissions and founder remuneration are not proven to be included. Consequently *operating surplus is not free cash flow*. Full annual cash-flow and runway cannot be certified.

## Decision gates (do not automatically approve commercial action)
1. **Demand proof:** Conduct structured interviews with relevant riders and dealers; secure dated, priced, cancellable/qualified paid-interest evidence. Separate leads and inquiries from sales or purchase orders.
2. **Price benchmark:** Obtain current like-for-like competitor pricing by size/specification, channel, warranty and freight. Verify SKU/date/region/currency and record supplier/reviewer identities.
3. **Landed BOM:** Reconcile carbon prepreg, layup labor, tooling, waste, quality testing, failure allowance, pack/freight, duties, tax treatment and channel contribution. Compare at 6/12/20 units and stress variable cost.
4. **Engineering release:** Clear outstanding carbon laminate/design/fork validation holds, independent test requirements, manufacturing process validation and traceable supplier qualification before customer product claims.
5. **Capital release:** Only after independently reconciled contribution and working-capital forecasts plus a pilot demand proof should management evaluate factory investment. Founder approval and separate statutory/engineering controls remain mandatory.
